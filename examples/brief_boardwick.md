- **Last uplift.** The last renewal uplift was 6%.

Saving estimates and the assumptions behind them:

- Unused seats: Keep 10% headroom over active seats (110 seats). The 10 seats above that cost $220 each per year. Low case recovers 50% of that gap, high case 100%.
- Overlap: Cost per seat is annual cost divided by seats purchased. Planhive is cheapest at $210. The estimate moves each other vendor's active seats onto Planhive at that rate and drops the other contract. It assumes Planhive will sell the extra seats at its current rate, which only a written quote can confirm. Low case counts 50% of the gap (migration cost, rate changes), high case 100%.
- These are estimates, not quotes. Nothing here is legal or financial advice.
